Help & support

Updated October 24, 2026

Get in touch

Email calinveres1@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, the 3Dit version (in Help & privacy), and what you tapped before the problem. Please don’t attach private room photos or scan exports unless you want to share them. When testing through TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback.

Supported devices

Scanning needs an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro with LiDAR, on iOS 17 or later. Other iPhones and iPads can open, view and share scans that were made on a LiDAR device.

Scanning a room

Create a project, tap Scan a room and name it. Move slowly in good light and keep walls and furniture in view; the guide tells you what’s left to cover. Finish when you’re happy, then open the room for the 3D model, measurements, inventory and the walkthrough video. Check critical measurements with a tape or laser before ordering or building.

Hiding and moving items

In the 3D view, tap an item to move, turn or hide it. The eye button on the right lists every item: show or hide them one at a time, or use Show all and Hide all. Hidden items stay hidden in every view.

Redesign

Open a room and tap Redesign in 3D. Tap a wall, the floor or an item to change it: paint, finishes, furniture and new walls. Keep several options, compare with Before, and share an image or the CAD files.

Exporting for CAD

Export for CAD creates a floor plan (DXF) for Vectorworks, AutoCAD and others, plus 3D models for SketchUp. Laser-corrected walls are marked (L).

Camera access is off

Open Help & privacy, then Open app settings, and turn on Camera. If it can’t be turned on, Screen Time or a device-management profile may be blocking it.

Importing a shared room

Save the 3Dit ZIP to Files and tap it to unzip. In 3Dit, tap the import button at the top left and pick the folder that contains manifest.json.

Recovering a deleted room

Deleted rooms go to Recently Deleted, below the project’s room list. Restore them from there or delete them permanently.
