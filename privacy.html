Privacy policy

Updated October 24, 2026

The short version

3Dit has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your scans, photos and measurements stay on your device. The developer never receives them, and the app has no server of its own.

What the app saves on your device

When you scan, 3Dit uses the camera and LiDAR to save room geometry, reference photos, a walkthrough video, depth frames and camera positions. It also saves what you add: project and room names, addresses, notes, inventory, laser measurements, plans you import, cable runs and design options. Technical details about each scan (device model, tracking quality, memory and temperature warnings) are saved with it so problems can be diagnosed. Photos and room geometry can show private details of your surroundings.

People in photos

To keep people out of models and renders, 3Dit looks for people, faces, hands and bodies in your scan photos and masks them out. This runs entirely on your device with Apple’s Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded, and the app doesn’t identify anyone.

Camera

Camera access is requested when you start a scan. You can turn it off in Settings. Viewing, importing and sharing existing scans doesn’t need it.

Location

Location is used only when you tap Use my location to fill in an address. Your position is sent to Apple’s Maps services to look up the street address and nearby places, and 3Dit saves only the address you keep. The Sites map also uses Apple Maps. Apple’s handling of this is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Photos library

3Dit asks to add to your Photos library only when you choose to save something there, such as a walkthrough video or design image. It never reads your library.

Sharing

Nothing is shared automatically. When you share a room, export for CAD or send a design, iOS sends the file to the app, person or device you pick. Exports can contain photos, video, measurements and scan diagnostics, so check the destination first. Copies you share are controlled by whoever receives them.

Purchases

If you buy 3Dit Pro, Apple handles the payment. 3Dit only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account and never sees your payment details.

Storage, backups and deletion

Scans are stored in the app’s Documents folder, which you can also reach through the Files app. Your device backups may include them. Deleted rooms go to Recently Deleted until you restore them or delete them permanently. Deleting a project removes its scans from the device. Backups and copies you’ve shared have to be removed separately.

Children

3Dit is a measuring tool for general audiences and doesn’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If 3Dit’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. Questions? Email calinveres1@gmail.com.
